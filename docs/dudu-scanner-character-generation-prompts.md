# Dudu Scanner character generation prompts

These prompts produced the first-round candidates for issue #134. The built-in
image generation path was used with a flat green chroma-key background, followed
by local background removal to RGBA PNG.

## Shared art direction

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production character illustration for a children's playful scanner web app
Primary request: Create one original, child-friendly character as a soft 2.5D cartoon sticker.
Style/medium: polished 2.5D digital cartoon illustration; rounded chunky silhouette; smooth clay-like forms with very gentle tactile texture; confident dark warm-gray outer outline of consistent medium weight; subtle internal linework; friendly simple face; premium mobile-game sticker quality.
Composition/framing: exactly one full-body character, centered, front three-quarter view, balanced compact proportions, generous even padding on every side, readable at 64 px.
Lighting/mood: soft upper-left studio light, gentle form shading contained inside the character, cheerful and reassuring.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background for background removal; one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation.
Consistency rules: character occupies about 68% of canvas height; same visual mass, eye style, outline weight, camera angle, lighting direction, and finish as a cohesive six-character family.
Constraints: opaque character with crisp closed silhouette; no cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection; no text, letters, numbers, logos, packaging brands, watermark, border, pedestal, props detached from character, gore, anatomy, medicine, realistic insect details, worms, parasites, organs, bodily fluids, or frightening features. Do not use #00ff00 anywhere in the character.
```

## Fry Sprite

```text
Character: Fry Sprite. A tiny happy bundle of 5 thick golden potato-fry batons forming a crown-like head, seated in a simple rounded coral-red carton-shaped body with no logo. Two stubby arms and two tiny sneaker-like feet integrated into the silhouette. Warm golden yellow and coral palette, rosy cheeks, bright curious smile. Clearly reads as a whimsical fry friend, not branded food packaging.
```

## Sleepy Bug
